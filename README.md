# 🎵 RhythmWave – Music Discovery

### Your Sound. Your Space.

RhythmWave is a modern and responsive music discovery website designed to help users explore music, discover popular artists, browse albums, and find curated playlists for every mood and moment.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://project-2-rhythmwave-vyshnavs.vercel.app/

## 🚀 Features

- **Featured Artists:** Discover popular artists like Arijit Singh, Anirudh Ravichander, Shreya Ghoshal, and Sid Sriram.
- **Albums & Collections:** Explore different music collections and albums.
- **Curated Playlists:** Discover playlists for coding, workouts, relaxation, and more.
- **Popular Tracks:** Browse a collection of popular songs.
- **Music Categories:** Explore music based on different moods and moments.
- **Modern UI:** Clean, attractive, and user-friendly interface.
- **Responsive Design:** Designed to provide a smooth experience across different screen sizes.
- **Easy Navigation:** Navigate between artists, albums, playlists, tracks, and about sections.

## 🎧 Music Categories

- Focus – Late Night Coding
- Workout – Morning Workout
- Relaxation – Easy Mornings
- Electronic – After Hours
- Indian Music – Deccan Groove
- Instrumental – Soft Focus

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Responsive Web Design

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of RhythmWave is to create an attractive and user-friendly music discovery platform that allows users to explore different music styles, artists, albums, and playlists in one place.
